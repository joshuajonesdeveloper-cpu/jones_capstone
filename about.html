About

Get to know the people-first approach behind Ironside Training Co. in Woodbridge, Virginia.

Our story

A stronger routine starts with feeling welcome.

Ironside began with a simple idea: training should make everyday life feel better. A gym does not need to be enormous to help someone build strength. It needs thoughtful coaching, useful equipment, and a place where questions are welcome.

Our small Woodbridge studio brings that idea to life through one-on-one coaching and small-group sessions. The coaches who lead each session help people learn movements, adjust exercises to their starting point, and recognize progress beyond the number on a barbell.

Whether you are picking up a weight for the first time or finding your way back to a routine, we help you take a manageable next step. You bring your goals; we bring a plan that fits real life.

What we believe

Good coaching goes beyond the workout.

Start where you are

Your first session should feel approachable. We explain the movements, listen to your questions, and build from your current abilities.

Coach the whole person

A training plan needs to fit work, family, rest, and real schedules. We focus on habits you can keep, with encouragement along the way.

Make steady progress

Small improvements add up. We celebrate better technique, growing confidence, and the consistency that makes strength useful outside the gym.

Our mission

Help people build lasting strength and confidence through clear coaching, realistic routines, and a community that makes room for every starting point.
